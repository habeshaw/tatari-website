ABOUT TATARI

Practical expertise.
Measurable progress.

TATARI Consulting is a management and technology advisory initiative based in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia. We bring together people management, information systems, analytics and technology to help organizations solve operational problems and make informed decisions.

Our approach is practical: understand the problem, agree on a useful deliverable, build it with the client, and transfer the knowledge needed to sustain it.
