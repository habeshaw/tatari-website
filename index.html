ABOUT TATARI

Practical expertise.

TATARI Consulting is an emerging management and technology consulting firm based in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia, helping organizations improve people management, business reporting, data-driven decision-making and practical digital transformation. We combine management expertise, human-resource management, information systems, and technology & innovation management with hands-on data analytics to provide affordable, practical and results-oriented solutions.
