Who We Are

እኛ ማን ነን

TATARI Consulting is an emerging management and technology consulting firm based in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia, helping organizations improve people management, business reporting, data-driven decision-making and practical digital transformation.

We combine management expertise, human-resource management, information systems, and technology & innovation management with hands-on data analytics to provide affordable, practical and results-oriented solutions — for organizations that can't justify (or don't want) a large traditional consulting firm.

ታታሪ ኮንሳልቲንግ በአዲስ አበባ፣ ኢትዮጵያ የሚገኝ አዳጊ የማኔጅመንት እና የቴክኖሎጂ ማማከር ድርጅት ሲሆን፣ ድርጅቶች የሰራተኛ አያያዝን፣ የንግድ ሪፖርት አቀራረብን፣ በመረጃ ላይ የተመሰረተ ውሳኔ አሰጣጥን እና ተግባራዊ ዲጂታል ለውጥን እንዲያሻሽሉ ይረዳል።
